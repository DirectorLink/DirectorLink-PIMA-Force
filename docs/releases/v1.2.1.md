# DirectorLink · PIMA FORCE 1.2.1

Free, open-source Control4 driver for PIMA FORCE alarm panels. Part of [DirectorLink Drivers](https://directorlink.io/drivers).

DirectorLink is an independent project, not affiliated with Control4, Snap One or PIMA.

## What's fixed

- **Arming and disarming from DirectorLink is quick again.** With 1.2.0 each command from DirectorLink 1.16.0 took about 15 seconds to reach the panel, and the app could say it had failed when it then went through. The driver ran under Control4's LuaJIT, where opening DirectorLink's sealed command was very slow on the controller. It now runs on standard Lua, as DirectorLink does: about a second.

## Updating

Download `DirectorLink-PIMA-Force.c4z` below, then in Composer **Driver → Add or Update Driver or Agent**. It updates 1.2.0 in place; its properties and key stay.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the package's checksum.
