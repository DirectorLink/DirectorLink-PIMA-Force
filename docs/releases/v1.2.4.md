# DirectorLink · PIMA FORCE 1.2.4

Free, open-source Control4 driver for PIMA FORCE alarm panels. Part of [DirectorLink Drivers](https://directorlink.io/drivers).

DirectorLink is an independent project, not affiliated with Control4, Snap One or PIMA.

## What's new

- **No more typing the panel's address.** **Panel IP Address** now shows the address the panel calls from, which the driver learns. If your router gives the panel a new address, after a power cut for example, the driver follows it and logs the move, instead of refusing the panel until someone changes the property.
- **Still safe:** while the panel is connected, a connection from any other address is refused, even with the panel's account number.
- A DHCP reservation for the panel in your router keeps its address from changing at all.

## Updating

Download `DirectorLink-PIMA-Force.c4z` below, then in Composer **Driver → Add or Update Driver or Agent**. It updates 1.2.x in place; the address you typed becomes the known one.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the package's checksum.
