# DirectorLink · PIMA FORCE 1.1.0

Free, open-source Control4 driver for PIMA FORCE alarm panels. Part of [DirectorLink Drivers](https://directorlink.io/drivers).

DirectorLink is an independent project, not affiliated with Control4, Snap One or PIMA.

## What's new

- **Shabbat mode:** arm for Shabbat from Control4 and DirectorLink, PIMA's own Shabbat arming (on panels sold as a "Shabbat system").
- **Quiet on Shabbat:** while armed for Shabbat, zones opening and closing are not passed to Control4, so people moving in the house cause nothing in its programming. An alarm is still reported, and the zones are reported again after Shabbat.
- **The full JSON setup** in the README and in Composer's Documentation tab, step by step as PIMA's guide gives it. Remote Disarm is under CMS & Communication → General Settings.
- **Tested with the JSON interface** on a FORCE 32C (firmware 3.0.19): the panel's zones read, and armed and disarmed from DirectorLink.

## Updating

Download `DirectorLink-PIMA-Force.c4z` below, then in Composer **Driver → Add or Update Driver or Agent**. It updates 1.0.0 in place: settings, connections and programming stay.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the package's checksum.
